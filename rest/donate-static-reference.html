Why Your Support Matters

We've filed many PRS complaints, public records requests and appeals, and regularly speak at school committee meetings. We track public records compliance across multiple districts, maintain a database of 7,800+ school-level restraint records, and publish data-driven reporting that families use to advocate for their children. This work is powered by APIs, hosting, and tools that cost real money every month. Your donation keeps this watchdog running.

Other Ways to Help

File Public Records Requests

The most powerful tool we have is the public records law. We need more people filing PRRs in their own districts — every request builds a paper trail and establishes patterns. We can help you draft and submit requests.

Request Help Filing a PRR →

Submit Documents & Data

Have school committee emails, IEP records, restraint reports, or other documents? Upload them securely — every document helps build the evidence record.

Speak at Public Comment

School committee meetings have public comment periods. Speaking up about special education, restraint policies, and transparency puts pressure on districts. We can help you prepare remarks.
